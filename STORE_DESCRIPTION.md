# BloxFront – Store-Beschreibung

## Titel
BloxFront

## Beschreibung (Englisch, für den Roblox-Store)

Grow from a single capital into a world power.

Pick your starting land, expand into neutral territory and outgrow every nation on the map. Hold 80% of the world to win.

⚔️ Attack your neighbours. Choose how many troops to send and push your borders.
🏙️ Build cities, ports, factories and defense posts to grow your economy.
🚢 Send boats across the sea and command warships.
🤝 Make alliances, or break them when the time is right.
☢️ Launch nukes from missile silos, and defend yourself with SAM launchers.
🌍 Play solo against AI nations on dozens of real-world maps, or join public matches and team games with friends.

Unlock weapons in the Arsenal, open crates for territory skins and collect flags for your nation.
